Guided experiments

reproducible comparisons
01 / STATIONARY

Which strategy spends exploration deliberately?

Keep the default stationary means, enable five-strategy comparison, and run 500 pulls. Compare UCB confidence scores, Thompson samples, and cumulative regret.

02 / DRIFT

What breaks when old evidence becomes stale?

Switch to non-stationary random walk after 500 pulls and continue. Observe which strategies revisit alternatives after the identity of the best arm changes.

03 / OPTIMISM

Can optimism create exploration without randomness?

Set ε=0 and Q₀=1.0 for ε-greedy, reset, and step manually. Each disappointing reward lowers one arm, causing an untried optimistic arm to be selected next.

  1. 01 / RANDOMNESSEpsilon-greedy explores at a fixed rate, so it keeps making random trials after finding a good arm.
  2. 02 / OPTIMISMUCB builds an optimistic bound from visit counts and prioritizes arms that remain uncertain.
  3. 03 / POSTERIORThompson sampling draws from a posterior and converts uncertainty directly into selection probability.
  4. 04 / DRIFTIn a drifting environment, sample averages remember stale rewards; compare which strategy keeps checking alternatives.
