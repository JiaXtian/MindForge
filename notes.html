A suggested recording rhythm

  1. Write the question you actually do not understand before copying a definition.
  2. State assumptions, symbol shapes, and the reason for every step in a derivation.
  3. Test the intuition with one reproducible experiment and keep failed results.
  4. Separate conclusions you understand from questions that remain open.
