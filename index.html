How to use the labs

Slow the loop down until it becomes legible.

Reinforcement learning becomes intuitive when every transition can be connected to one update, one changed estimate, and one later decision.

  1. 01

    Observe before training.

    Identify the state, available actions, reward, terminal condition, and information available to the agent.

  2. 02

    Change one variable.

    Adjust gamma, alpha, epsilon, or the environment while keeping the rest fixed, then compare trajectories.

  3. 03

    Record the surprise.

    A failed policy or unstable estimate is evidence about the algorithm's assumptions, not wasted motion.
